# Status Page Notifications

## Objective

The status page provides a full view of operations across four categories:

<ul class="status-orange-bullets">
	<li>Availability status over the past 90 days.</li>
	<li>Overview of upcoming planned maintenance.</li>
	<li>Communication during ongoing incidents, including impact and mitigation.</li>
	<li>A list of past incidents.</li>
</ul>

A notification service is available for subscribers and sends:

<ul class="status-orange-bullets">
	<li>Emails related to ongoing incidents.</li>
	<li>Reminder emails for upcoming maintenance.</li>
</ul>

Users can subscribe through the Subscribe to updates menu on the status page.

## How to subscribe

<ol class="status-subscribe-steps">
	<li>
		Go to <a href="https://itsme.statuspage.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">itsme.statuspage.io</a> and select <span class="status-keyword">Subscribe to updates</span>.
		<br />
		<img src="/doc/public/images/status-page-subscription-step1.png" alt="Status page subscription menu" style="max-width: 33%; height: auto;" />
	</li>
	<li>Enter your <span class="status-keyword">email address</span> and complete the captcha.</li>
	<li>A <span class="status-keyword">one-time password (OTP)</span> is sent to your email address.</li>
	<li>
		Open <span class="status-keyword">Subscribe to updates</span> again, then enter your email and OTP.
		<br />
		<img src="/doc/public/images/status-page-subscription-step2.png" alt="Status page OTP verification" style="max-width: 33%; height: auto;" />
	</li>
	<li>
		Select the services relevant to you and click <span class="status-keyword">Save</span>.
		<br />
		<img src="/doc/public/images/status-page-subscription-services-full.png" alt="Status page services selection" style="max-width: 33%; height: auto;" />
		<br />
		<img src="/doc/public/images/status-page-subscription-services-detail.png" alt="Status page services selection detail" style="max-width: 33%; height: auto;" />
	</li>
</ol>

## How to unsubscribe

<ol class="status-unsubscribe-steps">
	<li>Go to <a href="https://itsme.statuspage.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">itsme.statuspage.io</a>.</li>
	<li>Repeat the same steps as in the subscription flow.</li>
	<li>In the services list, unselect the services for which you no longer want notifications.</li>
</ol>

If you choose <span class="status-keyword">Select none</span> and save, you will be fully unsubscribed and stop receiving all notifications.
You can subscribe again later if needed.
